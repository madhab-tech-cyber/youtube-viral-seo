# YouTube Niche Catalog

Every major niche family on YouTube and the sub-niches creators actually build channels in. Use it when choosing or testing a niche: pick a family, shortlist sub-niches, then run each shortlisted one through the skill's three-legged test — proven demand, one-sentence differentiation, a hundred videos of depth.

**How to read the tiers.** RPM tier is *relative advertiser demand* (High / Medium / Low), not a dollar promise — real RPM swings with audience geography, season, video length and watch time, and Shorts earn a small fraction of long-form rates in every niche. Competition is relative saturation. Faceless marks whether the format works without an on-camera host. Verify demand against live channels before committing; this catalog is orientation, not data.

## 1. Finance & Business — RPM: High · Competition: High

Personal finance & budgeting · investing & stock market · index/retirement planning · cryptocurrency & Web3 · business case studies & company stories · startup & entrepreneurship · side hustles & making money online · real estate & property · frugal living & FIRE · tax & accounting basics · luxury business & billionaire stories · e-commerce & dropshipping · sales & marketing tactics. Faceless: yes (case studies, explainers, screen-based tutorials).

## 2. Technology — RPM: High · Competition: High

Gadget & phone reviews · laptop/PC reviews & builds · software tutorials · SaaS & app roundups · AI news · AI tools & tutorials · AI automation workflows · programming & coding tutorials · web/app development · cybersecurity & ethical hacking · tech explainers ("how X works") · smartphones & mobile ecosystems · smart home · EV & future tech · tech fails & history. Faceless: mostly yes (screen capture, narration, product footage).

## 3. Education & Science — RPM: Medium · Competition: Medium

Science explainers · physics & space · astronomy & universe · biology & evolution · chemistry · earth science & geology · ocean science · psychology explainers · philosophy · mathematics · trivia & general knowledge · language learning · exam prep & study skills · academic subject tutoring · "what if" science scenarios. Faceless: yes (animation and narration are the native format).

## 4. History & Society — RPM: Medium · Competition: Medium

History explainers · alternate history / what-if history · military & war history · ancient civilizations · medieval history · modern history & cold war · biography of historical figures · geography & maps · flags & countries compared · country/economy comparisons · demographics & population · linguistics & dead languages · mythology (also §22). Faceless: yes (map animation, illustration, narration).

## 5. True Crime & Mystery — RPM: Medium · Competition: High

Solved case breakdowns · unsolved mysteries · missing persons · cold cases · serial-killer documentaries · interrogation analysis · internet mysteries & lost media · disaster investigations · scam & fraud exposés · heists & escapes. Faceless: yes (narration over documents, photos, maps). Handle with care: real victims, real families — accuracy and taste are the channel's survival.

## 6. Horror & the Paranormal — RPM: Low–Medium · Competition: Medium

Creepypasta & horror-story narration · true scary stories (workplace, camping, home-alone) · analog horror · paranormal investigations · ghost & haunting stories · urban legends · creepy internet rabbit holes · horror-game lore · zombie & apocalypse survival stories · dystopia & what-if horror scenarios. Faceless: yes (narration-led; the voice is the brand).

## 7. Entertainment & Pop Culture — RPM: Low · Competition: High

Movie recaps & endings explained · TV show breakdowns · film theory & video essays · celebrity news & drama · award & red-carpet coverage · anime news, recaps & theory · K-pop & idol culture · meme reviews · viral-video commentary · reality-TV recaps · trailer reactions & breakdowns. Faceless: partially (commentary and recap formats yes; reaction formats usually need a face). Copyright discipline is existential here — transformative commentary, short clips, no re-uploads.

## 8. Gaming — RPM: Low–Medium · Competition: Very High

Let's plays & walkthroughs · game lore & story explained · speedrunning · esports news & highlights · game reviews & retrospectives · retro gaming · Minecraft · Roblox · GTA & open-world · shooters (COD, Valorant, Fortnite) · RPG & soulslike · horror games · mobile gaming · game development & devlogs · gaming news & industry drama · tips, builds & tier lists · gaming what-ifs & challenges. Faceless: yes (gameplay + voiceover is standard).

## 9. Health & Fitness — RPM: Medium–High · Competition: High

Home workouts & follow-alongs · strength & bodybuilding · weight loss journeys & advice · calisthenics · yoga, pilates & mobility · running & endurance · nutrition & meal planning · mental health & therapy topics · medical explainers & "what happens in your body" · sleep science · longevity & biohacking · physiotherapy & posture fixes. Faceless: partially (explainers yes; workout formats need a demonstrator — can be hired/cropped). Medical claims carry policy and safety risk: cite, disclaim, never diagnose.

## 10. Food & Cooking — RPM: Medium · Competition: High

Recipe channels (by cuisine) · 15-minute & budget meals · baking & desserts · street-food tours · restaurant reviews & hidden gems · chef techniques & skill videos · mukbang & eating shows · food science & "why" cooking · copycat fast-food recipes · meal prep · regional cuisine deep-dives · cooking ASMR (no-talk). Faceless: yes for hands-only, ASMR and narration formats.

## 11. Travel — RPM: Medium · Competition: High

Travel vlogs · city & country guides · budget/backpacking · luxury travel · hidden places & offbeat destinations · food travel · expat & living-abroad life · van life & RV · aviation, trains & transport geekery · hotel & airline reviews · travel tips & scams to avoid · walking tours (no narration). Faceless: partially (walking tours and guide-narration yes; vlogs are face-led by default).

## 12. Home, DIY & Lifestyle — RPM: Medium · Competition: Medium

DIY & home improvement · woodworking & making · home renovation & flips · interior design & small spaces · cleaning & organization · minimalism & decluttering · gardening & homesteading · tiny houses & alternative living · before/after transformations · furniture restoration · satisfying repairs. Faceless: yes (hands/process filming is the norm).

## 13. Animals & Nature — RPM: Low · Competition: Medium

Wildlife documentaries & animal facts · dangerous animals & apex predators · pet care & training (dogs, cats, exotics) · rescue & rehabilitation stories · funny pet compilations (original footage) · aquarium & terrarium builds · birding & backyard wildlife · insects & micro-world · nature scenery & ambient films. Faceless: yes (footage + narration).

## 14. Sports — RPM: Medium · Competition: High

Match analysis & tactics · football (soccer) stories & lists · cricket analysis & stories · NBA/basketball · NFL/American football · Formula 1 & motorsport · UFC/MMA & boxing · athlete biographies & documentaries · sports what-ifs & alternate timelines · sports business & transfers · highlights commentary · extreme & adventure sports. Faceless: yes for analysis/documentary formats. League footage is heavily claimed — analysis must be genuinely transformative and clip-light.

## 15. Motivation & Self-Improvement — RPM: Low–Medium · Competition: High

Stoicism & philosophy for life · discipline & habits · motivational speeches & edits · book summaries & key ideas · study techniques & student life · productivity systems & tools · social skills & communication · confidence & mindset · morning routines · monk-mode/deep-work content. Faceless: yes (narration + b-roll is the standard format). The niche punishes generic content — a specific angle or mechanism is the differentiation.

## 16. Stories, Books & Listening — RPM: Low–Medium · Competition: Medium

Reddit-story narration (AITA, confessions, revenge) · original short-story narration · bedtime stories for adults · sleep stories & calm narration · book summaries & reviews · literary analysis & author stories · poetry · audio drama · creepypasta (cross-listed §6) · true confessions & diary formats. Faceless: yes, entirely voice-led. Redistribution rules matter: narrating others' text needs transformation and permission discipline — original writing is the safe moat.

## 17. Music — RPM: Low · Competition: High

Covers & performance · instrument lessons & tutorials · music production & beat-making · lo-fi, chill & study beats (original) · ambient & focus playlists (original) · lyrics explained & song meanings · music history & artist documentaries · music theory · DJ sets & mixes (licensed) · reaction to music. Faceless: yes for lessons, production, ambient and documentary formats. Copyright is the whole game here — original or licensed, nothing else survives.

## 18. Comedy — RPM: Low · Competition: High

Sketch & skit comedy · animated comedy & storytime animation · commentary comedy · parody & satire · stand-up clips (own material) · roasts & panel formats · prank (heavily policy-sensitive) · meme compilations with original commentary · improv & podcast comedy clips. Faceless: partially (animation and commentary yes).

## 19. Kids & Family — RPM: Medium · Competition: High

Nursery rhymes & kids' songs · early learning (ABC, numbers, colours) · kids' story narration · toy unboxing & play · kids' science experiments · family vlogs · parenting tips · children's crafts & drawing. Faceless: yes (animation is dominant). Made-for-kids rules reshape everything: comments off, limited ads, different RPM — read the current policy before planning revenue.

## 20. News, Politics & Documentary — RPM: Medium · Competition: High

News explainers & breakdowns · geopolitics & international relations · war & conflict analysis · politics (national) commentary · economics explainers · mini-documentaries · investigative video essays · biography documentaries · disaster documentaries · engineering & megaproject documentaries · "how it's made" industrial content. Faceless: yes (the essay/documentary format is narration-native). Accuracy and impersonation policies apply with force in news-adjacent content.

## 21. Luxury, Extremes & Curiosity — RPM: Medium · Competition: Medium

Luxury lifestyles, homes, yachts & cars · richest people & net-worth stories · world records & extremes ("biggest, fastest, deepest") · abandoned places & urbex · mega machines & engineering feats · space missions & records · survival stories & against-all-odds · heists, escapes & capers (cross-listed §5) · odd history & forgotten events. Faceless: yes (compilation-documentary narration format).

## 22. Faith, Mythology & Spirituality — RPM: Low–Medium · Competition: Low–Medium

Devotional & prayer channels · sermons & scripture study · religious history · mythology retellings (Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Hindu…) · astrology & zodiac · tarot & readings · meditation & mindfulness · spiritual philosophy · comparative religion explainers. Faceless: yes (narration-led). Audience loyalty here is exceptional; claims and health-adjacent promises need the same care as §9.

## 23. ASMR, Relaxation & Satisfying — RPM: Low · Competition: Medium

ASMR triggers & roleplay · sleep sounds & rain/ambience · nature ambience films · oddly satisfying processes · soap cutting, kinetic sand & slime (original footage) · power-washing & restoration satisfying cuts · focus/study-with-me · calm narration & sleep stories (cross-listed §16). Faceless: yes, entirely. Low RPM is offset by extreme watch time and catalogue compounding.

## 24. Automotive & Transport — RPM: Medium–High · Competition: Medium

Car reviews & comparisons · supercars & hypercars · car history & model documentaries · restoration & project cars · racing coverage · EV transition content · truck, bike & aviation channels · train & ship documentaries · driving POV & road films. Faceless: partially (documentary and POV formats yes).

## 25. Regional, Language & Diaspora — a dimension, not a niche

Every family above exists in every language market, usually with less competition and lower RPM than English. Non-English and regional-language versions of proven English formats, diaspora-audience content, local history/geography/crime/finance, and dubbed or re-versioned original catalogues are a legitimate strategy axis: choose niche × language × geography together, and record all three in the channel profile.

## 26. Emerging & AI-Era — RPM: varies · Competition: forming

AI news & model coverage · AI tool workflows for professions · AI-generated story & animation channels (must still clear the reused-content/originality bar with real creative direction) · virtual influencers & VTubers · faceless automation case studies · prompt engineering & agent tutorials · synthetic-media explainers. Faceless: yes by construction. Fast-moving: what is empty this quarter is saturated next — speed and a specific audience are the whole edge.

---

*Choosing well matters more than choosing big: a Medium-RPM niche you can out-produce and out-last the field in beats a High-RPM niche you abandon in month two. Run the three-legged test, check the pick against the one-viewer promise, and write the result into `channel-profile.md`.*
